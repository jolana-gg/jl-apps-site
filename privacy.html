PRIVACY STATEMENT

This Privacy Statement ("Privacy Statement") describes how Jolana ("we", "us" and "our") handles personal information in connection with our applications (each, an "Application") and the website jl-apps.xyz (the "Website", and collectively with the Applications, the "Platform"). This Privacy Statement applies only to information collected through the Platform and explains in plain language what we do with that information, how we share it, and how we handle the content you place. It also explains your rights and the choices available to you regarding the use of your personal information and how you can access and update this information.

PERSONAL INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Our Applications are designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or have access to your personal data.

Information you provide to us

Personal information you may provide to us includes communications data based on our exchanges with you, including when you contact us through email or otherwise for support purposes.

We do not require account creation, registration, or login to use our Applications.

Information we do NOT collect

Unlike many applications, our Applications do NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information
  • Email addresses or contact information (unless you contact us)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Payment card data (one-time purchases, if any, are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details)

Automatic data collection

Our Applications do NOT automatically collect any data about you, your device, or your usage patterns. We do not use cookies, analytics frameworks, advertising identifiers, or any tracking technologies within our Applications.

Data storage

All data you enter into our Applications is stored:

  • Locally on your device - Your data is stored in the Application's local database on your device.
  • In your private cloud account - Cloud sync is on by default in some Applications (you can turn it off in the app). When it is on, your data is synchronized across your devices through your device provider's cloud service (for example, Apple iCloud). This data is stored in your private cloud container and is encrypted by the provider.

We do not have access to your cloud data. Your device provider manages cloud security, encryption, and privacy.

On-device permissions

Some Applications may ask for permission to use features on your device, such as Contacts, Photos, Calendar, or local notifications. If you import from Contacts, the fields you pick (for example name, photo, phone, birthday) are copied into the Application and then stored like anything else you enter: on the device and, if cloud sync is on, in your private cloud. Calendar access, if you turn it on, writes events to the Calendar app; those events may then sync through your device provider's Calendar/iCloud settings, separately from the Application's own cloud sync. Turning the Application's sync off does not remove those Calendar events. We do not receive this data.

HOW WE USE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION

Because we do not collect personal information through our Applications, we do not use your personal information for any purpose.

If you contact us for support, we may use your communication data to:

  • Respond to your requests, questions and feedback
  • Provide support for the Platform
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal process

HOW WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION

Because we do not collect personal information through our Applications, we do not share your personal information with any third parties.

If you contact us for support, we may share your communication data with:

  • Service providers. Third parties that provide services on our behalf, such as email delivery.
  • Legal authorities. Law enforcement and governmental authorities as we believe in good faith to be necessary or appropriate for compliance and protection purposes.

We do not sell your personal information.

YOUR CHOICES

Because we do not collect or store your personal information on our servers, you have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Access or update your information. You can view, modify, or export your data directly within the Application at any time. Some Applications omit certain items from export (for example, photos). Photos you add in the Application still stay on the device and, if cloud sync is on, in your private cloud.
  • Delete your data. Use the in-app delete option while cloud sync is still on — that removes local data and then syncs the deletion to your private cloud. Turning sync off, or deleting the Application, does not erase data already in your cloud account. If the Application is already gone, remove its data in your device's cloud settings (on iPhone: Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage).
  • Disable cloud sync. You can turn cloud sync off in the Application's settings.

INFORMATION SECURITY

Your data security depends on:

  • Your device security - Use a strong passcode or biometric authentication to protect your device.
  • Your cloud provider's security - If you use cloud sync, your data is protected by your provider's encryption and security measures.

We recommend enabling device encryption and using strong authentication methods to protect your data. We do not store Application data on our servers. If you email us (for example for support), we will have whatever you include in that message.

DATA RETENTION

We do not retain any of your data or personal information on our servers because we do not have servers that store user data.

If you contact us for support, we may retain your communication for as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry and for our records, after which it will be deleted.

RESIDENTS OF THE E.U.

Under the General Data Protection Regulations (GDPR), residents of the E.U. have the right to request the erasure of personal data, right to restrict processing, and in certain circumstances the right to data portability.

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, these rights are exercised directly through your device and cloud account, not through us. You have complete control over your data at all times through the Application.

Please direct any inquiries regarding the foregoing to contact@jl-apps.xyz.

RESIDENTS OF CALIFORNIA

If you are a California resident, under the California Consumer Privacy Act ("CCPA") you have additional rights regarding your personal information.

Right to Know

You have the right to know the categories of personal information we collect and the purpose for which we use it. As described above, we do not collect personal information through our Applications.

Right to Delete

You have the right to request that we delete the personal information we have collected from you. We do not store Application data on our servers, so use the in-app delete option while cloud sync is still on, and/or remove the Application's data in your device's cloud settings. Deleting the Application alone does not erase data already in your cloud account. If you have emailed us, ask us to delete that correspondence.

Right Not to Sell or Share Personal Information

The CCPA gives California residents the right to ask companies not to sell or share their personal information with third parties. We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Right Not to Be Discriminated Against

You have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising your consumer privacy rights under the CCPA.

If you are a California resident and would like to exercise your CCPA rights, please contact us at contact@jl-apps.xyz.
